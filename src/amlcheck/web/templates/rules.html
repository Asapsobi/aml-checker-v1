Rules: what each finding means

A check runs every rule below. Each rule that fires is a finding, and the most serious finding sets the verdict:

{{ verdict("BLOCK") }}A fact about the address itself (a sanctions listing, a Tether freeze). Do not transact.
{{ verdict("REVIEW") }}Look at it by hand before transacting: the finding says what to check.
{{ verdict("INCOMPLETE") }}A required source failed or is stale: the check couldn't finish. Retry, or treat as REVIEW.
{{ verdict("NO_HITS") }}Nothing was found in the sources checked. Not a clearance.

An INFO finding is shown and explains the score but never changes the verdict. The score (0–100) adds up everything: low 0–30, moderate 31–70, high 71–90, severe 91–100; from {{ review_at }} it is a REVIEW (R-SCR-01). The severities below are this installation's: the owner can change most of them in config.toml ([rules] severity).
